# Phase 03 – AI Risk Management

## Executive Summary

Following the successful establishment of the governance foundation and formal registration of GlobalBank Nexus, GlobalBank Corporation now enters the AI Risk Management phase of its Enterprise AI Governance Program.

Artificial Intelligence introduces unique operational, regulatory, security and ethical risks that extend beyond traditional technology risk management. These risks must be systematically identified, assessed, evaluated, treated and continuously monitored throughout the AI system lifecycle.

As part of the AI governance implementation engagement, **EthosSutra** has designed this phase to establish a structured, risk-based governance methodology for **GlobalBank Nexus**. The governance artifacts developed during this phase enable GlobalBank Corporation to understand the risks associated with the AI platform, determine appropriate governance controls and demonstrate effective risk management to executive leadership, regulators and auditors.

This phase transforms AI governance from documentation into active risk management and forms the operational core of the Enterprise AI Governance Program.

---

# Phase Objective

The objective of this phase is to establish a repeatable and auditable AI risk management process for GlobalBank Nexus.

This phase identifies the operational, technical, security, privacy, compliance and ethical risks associated with the AI system, evaluates their potential impact on the organization and defines appropriate treatment strategies.

Completion of this phase enables GlobalBank Corporation to implement governance controls proportionate to the identified risks while supporting responsible AI adoption across the enterprise.

---

# Consulting Engagement Context

Following the completion of AI Registration and Inventory, EthosSutra initiated a comprehensive AI risk assessment for GlobalBank Nexus.

The objective of this engagement is not only to identify AI-related risks but also to establish a governance methodology that can be consistently applied to future AI systems deployed across GlobalBank Corporation.

The governance artifacts produced during this phase provide a structured and evidence-based approach for managing AI risk throughout the operational lifecycle.

---

# Governance Artifacts

This phase establishes the enterprise AI risk management framework through the following governance artifacts.

| Governance Artifact | Purpose                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| AI Risk Assessment  | Identifies, evaluates and documents risks associated with GlobalBank Nexus.                            |
| Risk Register       | Maintains the official record of identified AI risks, ownership and treatment status.                  |
| Risk Treatment Plan | Defines mitigation strategies, governance controls and implementation activities for identified risks. |
| Risk Acceptance Log | Records formally approved residual risks accepted by authorized business stakeholders.                 |

Together, these artifacts provide the governance evidence required to demonstrate effective enterprise AI risk management.

---

# Governance Outcome

Upon completion of this phase, GlobalBank Corporation will have established:

* A comprehensive AI risk assessment.
* A centralized enterprise AI risk register.
* Documented mitigation and treatment strategies.
* Clearly assigned risk ownership and accountability.
* Formal risk acceptance procedures.
* Governance evidence supporting executive oversight and regulatory compliance.

These deliverables establish the foundation for governance controls, validation activities and operational monitoring in subsequent phases.

---

# Governance Rationale

Enterprise AI systems introduce risks that evolve continuously throughout their lifecycle.

Unlike traditional software, AI systems may exhibit model drift, hallucinations, bias, prompt injection vulnerabilities, data leakage, unauthorized actions and changing operational behaviour.

Effective AI governance therefore requires a structured and repeatable risk management methodology that extends beyond technical security controls.

By integrating AI risk management into the governance lifecycle, GlobalBank Corporation ensures that governance decisions remain informed, transparent and proportionate to the level of organizational risk.

---

# Framework Alignment

The governance activities performed during this phase support internationally recognized AI governance and risk management frameworks, including:

* NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Map, Measure and Manage Functions)
* ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System
* ISO 31000 Risk Management Principles
* EU AI Act Risk Management Requirements

Rather than performing independent assessments for each framework, GlobalBank Corporation maintains a single enterprise AI risk management process capable of supporting multiple governance and regulatory obligations.
