# Executive Summary

This document records the enterprise AI risk assessment performed for **GlobalBank Nexus** as part of GlobalBank Corporation's Enterprise AI Governance Program.

Following the successful registration and classification of the AI system, EthosSutra conducted a structured assessment to identify, evaluate and prioritize risks associated with the deployment and operation of GlobalBank Nexus.

The objective of this assessment is to determine whether the AI system can operate within GlobalBank Corporation's defined risk appetite while maintaining compliance with internal governance policies, regulatory obligations and responsible AI principles.

The assessment provides the foundation for governance controls, validation activities, operational monitoring and executive oversight throughout the AI system lifecycle.

# Assessment Scope

This assessment evaluates risks associated with the implementation and operation of GlobalBank Nexus across the following governance domains:

# Risk Assessment Methodology

GlobalBank Corporation applies a structured risk assessment methodology consisting of five sequential activities.

| Step | Activity                                            |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Identify AI-related risks                           |
| 2    | Assess likelihood and business impact               |
| 3    | Determine inherent risk level                       |
| 4    | Define governance controls and treatment actions    |
| 5    | Evaluate residual risk after control implementation |

This methodology ensures consistent and repeatable risk assessments across all enterprise AI systems.

# Overall Risk Evaluation

Following assessment, GlobalBank Nexus has been determined to represent a **High Enterprise AI Risk Profile**.

This classification reflects:

* Enterprise-wide deployment.
* AI-assisted decision support.
* Access to confidential banking information.
* Dependence on Large Language Models.
* Regulatory obligations within the financial services sector.
* Requirement for continuous governance oversight.

The identified risks are considered manageable through implementation of appropriate governance controls and ongoing operational monitoring.

# Key Assessment Findings

The assessment identified the following priorities:

* Human oversight is mandatory for AI-assisted business decisions.
* Enterprise monitoring must be implemented to detect abnormal AI behaviour.
* Prompt injection protection is required.
* Sensitive information controls must be enforced.
* AI outputs require validation before business reliance.
* Third-party AI services require ongoing governance.
* Continuous review of AI performance is necessary throughout the operational lifecycle.

These findings directly inform the governance controls implemented in subsequent phases.

---

# Governance Decision

Based on the results of this assessment, the AI Governance Board has approved GlobalBank Nexus to proceed with governance control implementation.

The system shall remain subject to:

* Governance policy enforcement.
* Human oversight.
* Validation and assurance.
* Continuous operational monitoring.
* Periodic risk reassessment.
* Executive governance reporting.

No production deployment shall occur outside the approved governance framework.
