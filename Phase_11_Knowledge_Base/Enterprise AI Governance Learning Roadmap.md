# Executive Summary

Enterprise AI Governance is one of the fastest growing disciplines within technology, risk management, compliance and cybersecurity.

Unlike traditional technology domains, AI Governance requires professionals to combine knowledge from artificial intelligence, enterprise architecture, information security, privacy, risk management, audit, legal compliance and organizational governance.

There is no single course, certification or book capable of developing complete AI Governance expertise.

This roadmap provides a structured learning journey that transforms foundational AI knowledge into practical enterprise governance capability through progressive learning, hands-on implementation and real-world governance artifacts.

The roadmap is based on the governance methodology implemented throughout the EthosSutra Enterprise AI Governance Reference Implementation and reflects how enterprise organizations design, implement, validate and continuously improve AI Governance programs.

The EthosSutra learning model follows a practical implementation-first approach.

```text

Build Governance Artifacts
        │
        ▼
Validate Implementation
        │
        ▼
```

Knowledge without implementation produces awareness.

Enterprise AI Governance requires mastery of both.

# International Standards Learning Path

Governance professionals should understand standards in the order they are typically applied during enterprise implementation.

| Standard | Why Learn It | Practical Outcome |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| NIST AI RMF | Learn AI risk management | Build AI Risk Assessment and Risk Register |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Learn AI Management Systems | Build an auditable AI Governance Program |
| EU AI Act | Understand regulatory obligations | Implement legal compliance controls |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Understand information security | Strengthen AI security governance |
| ISO/IEC 27701 | Understand privacy governance | Support privacy-by-design initiatives |

# Continuous Professional Development

Enterprise AI Governance is a rapidly evolving discipline. Professionals should establish an ongoing learning plan that includes:

- Monitoring emerging AI regulations.
- Reviewing updates to international standards.
- Participating in governance communities and industry forums.
- Following research from government agencies and standards bodies.
- Practising with new governance and AI evaluation tools.
- Contributing to governance documentation and implementation projects.
- Conducting periodic self-assessments against evolving competency requirements.

# Enterprise Learning Principles

The EthosSutra Enterprise AI Governance Learning Roadmap is based on the following principles:

- Learn governance through implementation rather than theory alone.
- Build practical artifacts that reflect real enterprise projects.
- Understand the intent behind international frameworks before applying them.
- Validate knowledge through testing, audit and operational experience.
- Develop multidisciplinary skills spanning governance, technology, risk, compliance and leadership.
- Treat learning as a continuous journey that evolves alongside AI technology and regulation.

Enterprise AI Governance is not mastered through a single course, certification or framework.

The EthosSutra Enterprise AI Governance Learning Roadmap provides a structured pathway from foundational AI knowledge to enterprise governance leadership, enabling professionals to design, implement, validate and continually improve AI Governance programs that are aligned with internationally recognized standards, regulatory expectations and real-world business needs.
