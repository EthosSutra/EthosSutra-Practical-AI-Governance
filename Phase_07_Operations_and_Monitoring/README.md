# Phase 07 – Operations and Monitoring

## Overview

This phase establishes the Enterprise AI Operations and Monitoring framework for **GlobalBank Nexus**.

Once an AI system has been deployed, governance responsibilities shift from implementation and validation to continuous operational oversight. AI systems require ongoing monitoring to ensure they continue operating safely, securely and in alignment with approved business objectives.

This phase provides the governance framework, operational procedures and supporting artifacts required to monitor AI performance, evaluate operational health, respond to incidents and demonstrate continuous governance throughout the production lifecycle.

Unlike traditional IT operations, AI Operations must continuously evaluate model behaviour, response quality, hallucinations, prompt injection attempts, privacy risks, governance compliance and business performance.

---

# Purpose

The Operations and Monitoring phase enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

- Continuously monitor AI system behaviour.
- Measure operational and governance performance.
- Detect operational anomalies before business impact occurs.
- Evaluate AI performance using standardized KPIs.
- Respond consistently to AI-related incidents.
- Produce audit-ready operational evidence.
- Support continuous improvement throughout the AI lifecycle.

# Operational Governance Domains

The Operations and Monitoring framework continuously evaluates GlobalBank Nexus across the following enterprise domains.

| Domain | Objective |
|----------|-----------|
| Operational Performance | Monitor system reliability, availability and responsiveness. |
| AI Quality | Evaluate response accuracy, groundedness and hallucination rates. |
| Security | Detect prompt injection, jailbreak attempts and unauthorized AI activities. |
| Privacy | Monitor protection of sensitive information and data governance controls. |
| Governance | Verify compliance with approved governance policies and human oversight requirements. |
| Business Performance | Measure AI adoption, user satisfaction and business value realization. |
