# Executive Summary

GlobalBank Nexus is the enterprise Artificial Intelligence platform developed by GlobalBank Corporation to support business operations, employee productivity, customer engagement and decision support across the organization.

The platform combines Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), enterprise knowledge retrieval and workflow automation to provide secure, governed and context-aware AI capabilities for banking operations.

As part of GlobalBank Corporation's enterprise AI transformation, GlobalBank Nexus has been identified as a strategically important AI platform requiring formal governance due to its ability to process enterprise information, support business decisions and influence operational outcomes.

This document establishes the official profile of GlobalBank Nexus and serves as the authoritative reference for all governance activities performed throughout this repository.

# Business Purpose

GlobalBank Nexus has been implemented to improve operational efficiency, enhance employee productivity and support informed decision-making across GlobalBank Corporation.

The platform provides secure access to enterprise knowledge, assists employees with complex business processes and delivers AI-assisted recommendations while maintaining appropriate governance controls and human oversight.

Rather than replacing business decision-makers, GlobalBank Nexus augments human expertise by providing timely, contextual and explainable assistance.

# Operational Scope

GlobalBank Nexus supports internal banking operations and controlled customer-facing services.

The platform is designed to assist employees by providing contextual information and recommendations while preserving human accountability for business decisions.

The platform does not independently approve loans, authorize financial transactions or execute customer-impacting decisions without human review.

# High-Level Risks

The operation of GlobalBank Nexus introduces risks including:

* Hallucinated AI responses.
* Prompt injection attacks.
* Unauthorized information disclosure.
* Model bias.
* Inaccurate decision support.
* Excessive user reliance on AI outputs.
* Regulatory non-compliance.
* Third-party AI dependencies.
* Data security risks.

These risks are formally assessed and managed through subsequent governance artifacts within this repository.
