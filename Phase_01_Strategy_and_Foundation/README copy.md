# Phase 01 – Strategy & Foundation

## Executive Summary

Every successful enterprise AI governance program begins with a clear understanding of the business, the AI system and the governance objectives that justify its deployment.

Before AI systems can be registered, risks assessed, controls implemented or compliance demonstrated, organizations must establish a shared understanding of the business context, governance scope, stakeholder accountability and technical architecture.

This phase establishes that foundation.

As part of the AI governance implementation engagement, **EthosSutra** works with **GlobalBank Corporation** to document the strategic, business and technical information required to govern **GlobalBank Nexus**, the bank's enterprise AI platform.

The governance artifacts produced during this phase establish the baseline for every subsequent governance activity and provide the evidence required to support enterprise AI governance throughout the system lifecycle.

---

# Phase Objective

The objective of this phase is to establish the strategic and operational foundation required to govern GlobalBank Nexus as an enterprise AI system.

This phase documents why the AI platform exists, the business value it delivers, the stakeholders responsible for its governance and the technical environment in which it operates.

Completion of this phase enables GlobalBank Corporation to proceed with AI registration, risk management, governance controls and regulatory compliance using a consistent and auditable governance foundation.

---

# Consulting Engagement Context

This repository represents a fictional enterprise consulting engagement.

**EthosSutra** has been engaged by **GlobalBank Corporation** to design and implement an enterprise AI governance program for **GlobalBank Nexus**, an AI platform supporting banking operations, customer services, internal knowledge management and decision support.

The governance artifacts contained within this repository represent the official deliverables produced throughout that engagement.

Each document contributes evidence demonstrating how enterprise AI governance is established, implemented and maintained within a financial services organization.

# Governance Outcome

Upon completion of this phase, GlobalBank Corporation will have established:

* A documented business context supporting the AI governance program.
* A formally defined enterprise AI system.
* Clearly identified governance stakeholders and accountability.
* A documented high-level AI system architecture.
* Defined governance scope and implementation boundaries.
* Foundational evidence supporting enterprise AI governance.

These deliverables enable the organization to progress confidently into AI registration, inventory management and risk assessment activities.

---

# Governance Rationale

Enterprise AI governance cannot begin with policies, controls or regulatory mappings.

Governance begins with understanding the organization, the AI system and the business objectives that drive its deployment.

Without this foundation, governance activities become inconsistent, difficult to audit and challenging to maintain across the AI lifecycle.

The governance artifacts produced during this phase establish the contextual evidence required to support governance decisions, risk management, oversight activities and regulatory compliance.

---

# Framework Alignment

The governance activities performed during this phase support foundational requirements defined within internationally recognized AI governance frameworks, including:

* NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Govern Function)
* ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System
* EU AI Act Governance Principles

Rather than replicating framework requirements, this phase demonstrates how those principles are implemented within an enterprise AI governance program.
