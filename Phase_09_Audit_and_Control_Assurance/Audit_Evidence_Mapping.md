# Executive Summary

Enterprise AI Governance must be supported by objective, verifiable and traceable evidence.

Policies, procedures and governance controls alone are insufficient unless the organization can demonstrate that they have been implemented, operated and monitored consistently.

The Audit Evidence Mapping Framework establishes a centralized approach for linking governance risks, controls, operational evidence, repository artifacts, enterprise systems and accountable owners.

This document enables Internal Audit, Regulatory Compliance and Executive Management to quickly identify the evidence required to validate governance activities across the lifecycle of GlobalBank Nexus.

It also reduces audit preparation effort by providing a standardized evidence catalogue that supports regulatory inspections, internal assurance activities and continuous governance monitoring.

# 13. Evidence Validation Framework

Every evidence item shall be independently validated before it is accepted for audit purposes.

| Validation Activity | Purpose |
|---------------------|---------|
| Authenticity Verification | Confirm evidence originates from an approved source |
| Completeness Review | Ensure all required information is present |
| Accuracy Verification | Confirm information reflects actual operational activity |
| Timestamp Validation | Verify evidence relates to the correct reporting period |
| Integrity Verification | Confirm evidence has not been modified |
| Traceability Check | Confirm linkage to risks, controls and governance artifacts |

# 18. Enterprise Best Practices

| Best Practice | Business Value |
|--------------|----------------|
| Collect evidence continuously rather than immediately before audits | Maintains continuous audit readiness |
| Standardize evidence naming conventions | Improves traceability and retrieval |
| Automate evidence collection where practical | Reduces manual effort and human error |
| Protect evidence with RBAC and encryption | Preserves confidentiality and integrity |
| Link every evidence item to risks, controls and governance artifacts | Strengthens governance transparency |
| Periodically validate evidence quality | Ensures evidence remains reliable and audit-ready |

---

# 19. Lessons Learned

| Observation | Recommendation |
|------------|----------------|
| Missing evidence is often treated as missing controls during audits | Collect evidence as part of normal operations |
| Screenshots alone rarely satisfy enterprise audits | Supplement with logs, reports and system-generated records |
| Evidence scattered across multiple systems delays audits | Centralize evidence within an enterprise repository |
| Manual evidence collection is difficult to sustain | Automate evidence generation where possible |
| Strong traceability improves regulatory confidence | Maintain end-to-end links between risks, controls, evidence and governance decisions |
