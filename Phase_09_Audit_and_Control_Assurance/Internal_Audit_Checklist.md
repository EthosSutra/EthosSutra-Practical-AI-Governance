# Executive Summary

The Internal AI Governance Audit Checklist establishes a standardized framework for independently evaluating the effectiveness of governance controls implemented across GlobalBank Nexus.

The checklist enables Internal Audit to assess governance maturity, verify compliance with enterprise policies, validate operational controls and determine whether governance activities produce sufficient evidence to satisfy regulatory, contractual and organizational expectations.

Unlike operational monitoring, this checklist provides an independent assurance mechanism that evaluates whether governance controls are properly designed, consistently implemented and operating effectively throughout the AI lifecycle.

This document supports Internal Audit, Executive Management, AI Governance Committees and Regulatory Examiners by providing a repeatable and evidence-driven audit approach.

The purpose of this checklist is to provide Internal Audit with a structured methodology for evaluating AI governance implementation across GlobalBank Nexus.

# 5. Enterprise Audit Principles

Every Internal AI Governance Audit shall follow these principles.

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Independence | Audit activities shall remain independent from operational teams. |
| Objectivity | Conclusions shall be based on verifiable evidence. |
| Risk-Based | Higher-risk governance areas receive greater audit attention. |
| Traceability | Every audit conclusion shall be supported by documented evidence. |
| Repeatability | Audit procedures shall produce consistent results across review cycles. |
| Confidentiality | Audit information shall be protected according to enterprise policies. |

# 18. Enterprise Best Practices

| Best Practice | Business Value |
|--------------|----------------|
| Perform audits using a risk-based approach | Focuses assurance efforts on the highest-risk AI capabilities |
| Validate operational evidence instead of relying solely on documentation | Improves confidence in governance effectiveness |
| Sample AI decisions from real production activity | Confirms controls operate under actual business conditions |
| Include technical, business and governance stakeholders in interviews | Provides a complete view of governance maturity |
| Track audit findings to verified closure | Ensures continuous improvement rather than one-time reporting |
| Benchmark audit results across review cycles | Measures governance maturity over time |

---

# 19. Lessons Learned

| Observation | Recommendation |
|------------|----------------|
| AI governance changes more rapidly than traditional IT governance | Increase audit frequency for critical AI systems |
| Automated controls still require independent validation | Include technical testing within every audit cycle |
| Governance documentation alone does not prove operational effectiveness | Validate implementation using logs, workflows and system-generated evidence |
| Cross-functional participation improves audit quality | Include AI Engineering, Risk, Privacy, Security and Business Owners |
| Continuous assurance reduces audit preparation effort | Integrate audit activities with ongoing monitoring and evidence collection |
