# Phase 09 – Audit and Control Assurance

## Overview

Enterprise AI Governance is only effective when governance activities can be independently verified.

Policies establish expectations.

Risk assessments identify exposure.

Controls reduce risk.

Monitoring provides operational visibility.

However, governance cannot be considered complete until an independent function can verify that controls operate as intended and that sufficient evidence exists to demonstrate compliance.

Phase 09 establishes the Enterprise Audit and Control Assurance Framework for GlobalBank Corporation.

The framework provides a structured approach for managing governance controls, validating their effectiveness, maintaining audit-ready evidence and conducting independent assurance activities across the complete lifecycle of GlobalBank Nexus.

Rather than treating audits as periodic compliance exercises, this phase embeds continuous assurance into day-to-day AI governance operations, ensuring the organization remains prepared for executive reviews, regulatory inspections and internal audits at any time.

---

# Purpose

The Audit and Control Assurance phase enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

- Maintain a centralized inventory of AI governance controls.
- Verify that governance controls operate effectively.
- Produce objective and traceable audit evidence.
- Support independent internal audit activities.
- Demonstrate regulatory compliance.
- Improve governance maturity through continuous assurance.
- Strengthen executive confidence in AI governance effectiveness.

# Audit and Assurance Domains

The framework evaluates governance effectiveness across the following enterprise domains.

| Domain | Objective |
|--------|-----------|
| Governance Controls | Verify governance policies and control implementation. |
| Risk Management | Confirm AI risks are appropriately identified, assessed and treated. |
| Human Oversight | Validate mandatory human review for high-risk AI activities. |
| AI Lifecycle | Verify model governance, validation and deployment controls. |
| Data Governance | Confirm data quality, lineage, classification and protection. |
| Information Security | Validate access management, security controls and operational resilience. |
| Privacy & Compliance | Verify protection of sensitive information and regulatory compliance. |
| Third-Party Governance | Assess governance of external AI suppliers and service providers. |
| Monitoring & Operations | Confirm continuous monitoring and operational assurance. |
| Audit & Evidence | Validate traceability, evidence quality and governance assurance activities. |

# Core Governance Principles

This phase is built upon the following enterprise principles:

- Every governance control shall have a clearly defined owner.
- Every control shall be periodically tested for effectiveness.
- Every governance activity shall produce objective audit evidence.
- Every audit conclusion shall be supported by traceable evidence.
- Audit activities shall remain independent from operational ownership.
- Governance findings shall result in measurable corrective actions.
- Continuous assurance is more effective than periodic compliance exercises.
