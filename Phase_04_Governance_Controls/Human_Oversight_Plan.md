# Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence enhances decision-making by analyzing information at speed and scale. However, accountability for business decisions remains with GlobalBank Corporation and its authorized personnel.

GlobalBank Nexus is designed to support employees through knowledge retrieval, analysis and decision support. It is not authorized to make autonomous business decisions that replace human judgement or organizational accountability.

This Human Oversight Plan establishes the governance framework for ensuring that AI-assisted activities remain subject to appropriate human supervision, intervention and accountability throughout the AI lifecycle.

The plan defines how oversight activities are performed, who is responsible for oversight, when human intervention is required and how oversight activities are documented to support governance, regulatory compliance and audit assurance.

Human oversight is a core component of the Enterprise AI Governance Program and applies throughout the design, deployment, operation and continuous improvement of GlobalBank Nexus.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Human Oversight Plan is to establish a structured governance approach for ensuring that AI-assisted business activities remain subject to meaningful human review and accountability.

This plan enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

* Maintain human accountability for AI-assisted decisions.
* Reduce operational and regulatory risk.
* Support responsible AI adoption.
* Prevent inappropriate reliance on AI-generated outputs.
* Ensure significant business decisions remain subject to authorized human judgement.
* Establish consistent oversight practices across all business functions.
* Demonstrate compliance with enterprise governance requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

This plan applies to all AI-assisted business activities performed through GlobalBank Nexus.

It applies to:

* Business users.
* Managers.
* AI Governance personnel.
* Risk Management.
* Compliance.
* Information Security.
* Internal Audit.
* Authorized third parties acting on behalf of GlobalBank Corporation.

Human oversight requirements apply regardless of whether AI recommendations are generated automatically or through user interaction.

The requirements defined within this plan shall be applied throughout the complete AI lifecycle.

# 4. Human Oversight Principles

The following principles govern every AI-assisted business activity performed through GlobalBank Nexus.

---

## HO-001 — Human Accountability

Artificial Intelligence shall assist human decision-making but shall not assume organizational accountability for business outcomes.

---

## HO-002 — Meaningful Human Review

Human oversight shall represent an informed and independent evaluation of AI-generated outputs rather than a routine approval activity.

---

## HO-003 — Right to Challenge

Authorized reviewers shall have the authority to question, reject or request clarification of AI-generated recommendations.

---

## HO-004 — Right to Override

Authorized personnel shall be empowered to modify, suspend or reject AI recommendations whenever business judgement indicates an alternative course of action.

---

## HO-005 — Escalation

Where AI recommendations create uncertainty, material business impact or elevated risk, the matter shall be escalated through the established governance process.

---

## HO-006 — Competency

Individuals performing oversight activities shall possess appropriate business knowledge, governance awareness and understanding of AI limitations.

---

## HO-007 — Documentation

Material oversight decisions shall be documented to support governance transparency, accountability and audit readiness.

---

## HO-008 — Continuous Improvement

Human oversight practices shall be periodically evaluated and enhanced based on governance reviews, incidents, monitoring activities and lessons learned.

# 5. Human Oversight Operating Model

GlobalBank Corporation applies a structured Human Oversight Operating Model to ensure that AI-assisted business activities remain subject to appropriate human judgement throughout the AI lifecycle.

The operating model establishes when oversight is required, who performs oversight and how oversight activities are executed and documented.

Human oversight is integrated into business operations rather than performed as an isolated governance activity.

The Human Oversight Operating Model consists of five core components.

| Component     | Purpose                                                                                  |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Review        | Evaluate AI-generated recommendations before business reliance where required.           |
| Challenge     | Critically assess AI outputs using professional judgement and business expertise.        |
| Intervention  | Modify, suspend or reject AI recommendations when appropriate.                           |
| Escalation    | Refer high-risk or uncertain AI outcomes to the appropriate governance authority.        |
| Documentation | Record material oversight decisions to support governance transparency and auditability. |

---

# 6. Human Oversight Roles and Responsibilities

Effective human oversight requires clearly defined ownership across business, governance and technical functions.

---

## 6.1 Business Decision Maker

The Business Decision Maker remains accountable for the final business outcome.

Responsibilities include:

* Reviewing AI-generated recommendations.
* Applying professional judgement.
* Considering relevant business context.
* Approving, modifying or rejecting AI recommendations.
* Escalating significant concerns where appropriate.

Business accountability shall not be delegated to Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 6.2 Business Manager

Business Managers shall:

* Ensure oversight activities are consistently performed.
* Verify staff understand oversight responsibilities.
* Review material oversight decisions.
* Escalate recurring governance concerns.

---

## 6.3 AI Governance Office

The AI Governance Office shall:

* Maintain the Human Oversight Framework.
* Define oversight requirements.
* Monitor oversight effectiveness.
* Review governance metrics.
* Recommend continuous improvements.

---

## 6.4 Risk Management

Risk Management shall:

* Assess AI-related business risks.
* Determine where enhanced oversight is required.
* Review high-risk oversight decisions.
* Support governance escalation.

---

## 6.5 Compliance

Compliance shall:

* Interpret applicable regulatory obligations.
* Monitor adherence to oversight requirements.
* Support regulatory examinations.

---

## 6.6 Internal Audit

Internal Audit shall independently evaluate:

* Effectiveness of oversight activities.
* Documentation quality.
* Governance compliance.
* Opportunities for improvement.

# 7. Human Oversight Decision Framework

Human oversight requirements shall be determined using a risk-based approach.

The level of oversight shall consider:

* Business impact.
* Regulatory obligations.
* Information sensitivity.
* Financial impact.
* Customer impact.
* Operational risk.
* Confidence in AI-generated outputs.
* Potential consequences of incorrect decisions.

Higher-risk activities require stronger human oversight.

---

# 8. Levels of Human Oversight

GlobalBank Corporation establishes four levels of human oversight.

| Level             | Oversight Requirement                                                    | Example                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Level 1 – Inform  | AI provides information only. Human review is optional.                  | Internal knowledge search.                                                                  |
| Level 2 – Review  | Human reviews AI output before business use.                             | Draft reports or policy summaries.                                                          |
| Level 3 – Approve | Human approval is mandatory before business execution.                   | Customer communications, compliance advice or financial analysis.                           |
| Level 4 – Govern  | Executive or specialized governance review is required before execution. | High-risk decisions involving customers, regulatory reporting or material financial impact. |

Business functions shall determine the appropriate oversight level during AI use case assessment.

---

### HO-009 — Risk-Based Oversight

The level of human oversight shall be proportionate to the business, operational and regulatory risk associated with the AI-assisted activity.

---

### HO-010 — Mandatory Approval

AI-assisted activities designated as requiring Level 3 or Level 4 oversight shall not proceed without the required human approval.

---

# 9. Human Review Process

Individuals performing oversight shall conduct a structured review before relying on AI-generated outputs where oversight is required.

The review shall consider:

* Whether the AI response addresses the intended business objective.
* Accuracy and completeness of the information.
* Consistency with enterprise policies.
* Potential bias or inappropriate assumptions.
* Availability of supporting evidence.
* Business context not available to the AI.
* Potential customer, financial or regulatory impact.

Human reviewers shall document material decisions where required by governance procedures.

---

### HO-011 — Professional Judgement

Human reviewers shall apply independent professional judgement and shall not rely solely on AI confidence scores or system-generated recommendations.

---

### HO-012 — Evidence-Based Review

Where practical, material AI recommendations shall be validated using appropriate business evidence before implementation.

# 10. Human Intervention

Human intervention shall occur whenever an authorized reviewer determines that an AI-generated recommendation may be inaccurate, incomplete, inappropriate or inconsistent with business, legal or regulatory requirements.

Intervention is intended to prevent inappropriate reliance on Artificial Intelligence and to ensure that business decisions remain subject to informed human judgement.

Examples of circumstances requiring intervention include:

* AI recommendations that conflict with business evidence.
* AI outputs containing factual inaccuracies.
* Recommendations affecting customers, employees or regulated activities.
* Potential bias or unfair outcomes.
* Recommendations exceeding delegated authority.
* Situations where the reviewer lacks sufficient confidence in the AI output.

Human intervention shall occur before implementation of any AI-assisted activity requiring oversight.

---

### HO-013 — Mandatory Human Intervention

Authorized reviewers shall intervene whenever AI-generated outputs create material business, operational, legal or regulatory concerns.

---

### HO-014 — Independent Decision-Making

Human reviewers shall exercise independent professional judgement and shall not approve AI recommendations solely because they were generated by GlobalBank Nexus.

---

# 11. Override Authority

GlobalBank Corporation establishes formal authority for designated personnel to modify, reject or suspend AI-generated recommendations.

Override authority shall be exercised responsibly and supported by appropriate business justification.

Where an AI recommendation is overridden, the reviewer shall document the rationale for the decision in accordance with applicable governance procedures.

The ability to override AI recommendations shall not be restricted where immediate action is necessary to protect customers, employees, business operations or regulatory compliance.

---

### HO-015 — Right to Override

Authorized personnel shall have the authority to reject, modify or suspend AI-generated recommendations within their delegated business authority.

---

### HO-016 — Documented Overrides

Material overrides shall include documented business justification supporting the final decision.

---

# 12. Human Authority Hierarchy

Oversight authority shall align with the significance of the AI-assisted activity.

The following hierarchy establishes minimum oversight expectations.

| Decision Type                      | Minimum Oversight Authority |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| General knowledge retrieval        | Business User               |
| Internal operational guidance      | Team Manager                |
| Customer-facing communications     | Business Manager            |
| Financial or operational approvals | Authorized Business Owner   |
| High-risk customer decisions       | Senior Business Owner       |
| Regulatory reporting               | Executive Management        |
| Material governance exceptions     | AI Governance Committee     |

Business functions may implement more restrictive oversight requirements where appropriate.

---

### HO-017 — Delegated Authority

Human oversight decisions shall only be performed by individuals operating within their approved delegated authority.

---

# 13. Escalation Framework

Where AI-generated recommendations create uncertainty, disagreement or elevated business risk, the matter shall be escalated through the Enterprise AI Governance Framework.

Escalation may be required when:

* Human reviewers disagree with AI recommendations.
* AI recommendations conflict with organizational policies.
* Significant customer impact is identified.
* Regulatory obligations may be affected.
* Multiple oversight reviewers reach different conclusions.
* Repeated AI performance concerns are identified.

Escalation activities shall be documented and resolved through the appropriate governance authority.

---

### HO-018 — Mandatory Escalation

Material disagreements regarding AI-assisted decisions shall be escalated in accordance with approved governance procedures.

---

### HO-019 — Governance Review

Recurring oversight concerns shall be referred to the AI Governance Office for assessment and continuous improvement.

---

# 14. Oversight Documentation

Human oversight activities shall be documented where required to support governance transparency, operational accountability and audit readiness.

Oversight documentation may include:

* Reviewer identity.
* Date and time of review.
* AI-assisted activity.
* AI recommendation.
* Final human decision.
* Business justification.
* Supporting evidence.
* Escalation details where applicable.

Documentation shall be retained in accordance with enterprise records management requirements.

---

### HO-020 — Oversight Record

Material oversight decisions shall be documented to provide a complete and traceable record of the review process.

---

### HO-021 — Evidence Retention

Oversight records shall be retained in accordance with applicable legal, regulatory and organizational requirements.

---

# 15. Oversight Monitoring

GlobalBank Corporation shall continuously monitor the effectiveness of Human Oversight activities.

Monitoring activities may include:

* Review completion rates.
* AI override frequency.
* Escalation trends.
* Repeat AI recommendations requiring intervention.
* Timeliness of oversight activities.
* Oversight quality reviews.
* Internal audit observations.
* User feedback.

Monitoring results shall be used to strengthen governance practices and improve AI performance over time.

---

### HO-022 — Oversight Effectiveness

The AI Governance Office shall periodically evaluate the effectiveness of Human Oversight activities using defined governance metrics.

---

### HO-023 — Continuous Monitoring

Human Oversight shall remain subject to continuous governance monitoring and periodic review throughout the AI lifecycle.

# 17. Competency, Training and Awareness

Human oversight is only effective when performed by individuals with the appropriate knowledge, experience and authority.

GlobalBank Corporation shall ensure that individuals responsible for oversight activities receive appropriate education and ongoing professional development.

Training shall include:

* AI Governance principles.
* Human Oversight responsibilities.
* AI capabilities and limitations.
* Recognition of AI bias and hallucinations.
* Responsible decision-making.
* Regulatory obligations.
* Escalation procedures.
* Documentation requirements.

Training shall be completed before individuals perform oversight responsibilities and refreshed periodically thereafter.

---

### HO-026 — Mandatory Competency

Individuals performing Human Oversight activities shall demonstrate the knowledge and competency required to execute their responsibilities effectively.

---

### HO-027 — Continuing Education

Oversight personnel shall participate in periodic refresher training to maintain awareness of evolving AI technologies, governance practices and regulatory requirements.

# Human Accountability Statement

Artificial Intelligence can accelerate analysis, identify patterns and provide decision support, but it cannot assume responsibility for the business outcomes of GlobalBank Corporation.

Every AI-assisted decision remains the responsibility of authorized personnel acting within their delegated authority and exercising informed professional judgement.

Human Oversight is not a procedural checkpoint. It is a governance capability that preserves accountability, protects customers, reduces organizational risk and reinforces trust in the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

This Human Oversight Plan establishes the enterprise standard for ensuring that AI remains a tool that supports human expertise rather than replacing it.
