# Executive Summary

GlobalBank Nexus is an enterprise Artificial Intelligence platform designed to enhance employee productivity, support informed decision-making and improve operational efficiency across GlobalBank Corporation.

The responsible use of Artificial Intelligence is essential to protecting customer trust, safeguarding confidential information and maintaining compliance with applicable legal, regulatory and organizational requirements.

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes the mandatory requirements governing the appropriate use of GlobalBank Nexus by all authorized users.

The policy defines acceptable and prohibited uses of the AI system and establishes the responsibilities of employees, contractors and third parties when interacting with enterprise AI capabilities.

Failure to comply with this policy may result in suspension of AI access, disciplinary action or other corrective measures in accordance with GlobalBank Corporation policies.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that GlobalBank Nexus is used responsibly, securely and consistently across the organization.

This policy establishes clear expectations regarding acceptable user behaviour while reducing operational, security, privacy and regulatory risks associated with enterprise AI usage.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

* All employees.
* Contractors.
* Consultants.
* Temporary staff.
* Third-party service providers.
* Any individual authorized to access GlobalBank Nexus.

The policy applies regardless of the device, network or location from which GlobalBank Nexus is accessed.

---

# 3. Policy Objectives

This policy is intended to:

* Promote responsible AI usage.
* Protect confidential and regulated information.
* Reduce security and compliance risks.
* Prevent inappropriate or unauthorized use of AI.
* Maintain customer trust.
* Support responsible business decision-making.
* Ensure compliance with the Enterprise AI Governance Program.

---

# 4. Authorized Use

Authorized users may use GlobalBank Nexus only for legitimate business purposes that support GlobalBank Corporation's approved business activities.

Examples of acceptable use include:

* Researching internal policies and procedures.
* Summarizing approved business documentation.
* Drafting business communications.
* Supporting operational decision-making.
* Improving employee productivity.
* Generating business insights using approved enterprise data.
* Assisting with knowledge discovery across authorized information sources.

Users shall ensure that all AI-generated content is reviewed for accuracy before being relied upon for business purposes.

---

# 5. Prohibited Use

The following activities are strictly prohibited.

Users shall not:

* Upload confidential information into unauthorized AI services.
* Attempt to bypass security or governance controls.
* Use AI to generate fraudulent, misleading or deceptive content.
* Use AI for harassment, discrimination or inappropriate communications.
* Generate content that violates laws, regulations or GlobalBank policies.
* Attempt to manipulate AI system behaviour through malicious prompts.
* Use AI to make unauthorized financial, legal or employment decisions without required human review.
* Share AI-generated confidential information with unauthorized individuals.
* Circumvent established approval or governance processes.

---

# 6. User Responsibilities

Every authorized user is responsible for ensuring that AI is used responsibly and in accordance with this policy.

Users shall:

* Protect confidential information.
* Verify AI-generated outputs before use.
* Exercise professional judgement.
* Report suspected AI errors or governance concerns.
* Complete mandatory AI governance training.
* Comply with all related organizational policies.

Use of GlobalBank Nexus does not remove individual accountability for business decisions.

---

# 7. Information Protection

Users shall ensure that information entered into GlobalBank Nexus is handled in accordance with GlobalBank Corporation's Data Handling Guidelines and Information Classification Policy.

Users shall not:

* Upload highly restricted information unless explicitly authorized.
* Share customer information beyond approved business purposes.
* Process regulated data outside approved governance controls.
* Store AI-generated outputs in unauthorized locations.

---

# 8. Human Review

Artificial Intelligence is intended to assist—not replace—human decision-making.

Users shall:

* Review AI-generated recommendations before use.
* Validate factual accuracy where appropriate.
* Escalate unusual or unexpected AI behaviour.
* Apply professional judgement before acting on AI outputs.

Business accountability always remains with the authorized employee.

---

# 9. Security Requirements

Authorized users shall:

* Protect their credentials.
* Access GlobalBank Nexus only through approved enterprise channels.
* Report suspected security incidents immediately.
* Comply with enterprise cybersecurity requirements.
* Never attempt to disable or bypass implemented security controls.

---

# 10. Monitoring and Compliance

Use of GlobalBank Nexus may be monitored for the purposes of:

* Information security.
* Operational monitoring.
* Governance oversight.
* Regulatory compliance.
* Incident investigation.
* Internal audit.

Monitoring activities shall be conducted in accordance with applicable laws and GlobalBank Corporation policies.

---

# 11. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

* Temporary suspension of AI access.
* Mandatory retraining.
* Governance review.
* Disciplinary action.
* Contractual remedies for third parties.
* Regulatory reporting where required.
