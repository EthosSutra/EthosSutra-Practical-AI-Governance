# Executive Summary

Effective AI governance depends upon clearly defined ownership, accountability and collaboration.

The Enterprise AI Governance RACI Matrix establishes responsibility assignments for key governance activities supporting GlobalBank Nexus throughout its lifecycle.

This document ensures that governance activities are consistently executed by identifying who is:

* Responsible for performing the activity.
* Accountable for the outcome.
* Consulted before decisions are made.
* Informed after decisions are completed.

The RACI Matrix supports effective decision-making, eliminates ambiguity and strengthens accountability across the Enterprise AI Governance Program.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define governance responsibilities for the implementation, operation and continuous improvement of GlobalBank Corporation's Enterprise AI Governance Program.

The matrix supports:

* Clear accountability.
* Effective decision-making.
* Cross-functional collaboration.
* Consistent governance execution.
* Regulatory compliance.
* Audit readiness.

---

# 2. How to Use This Matrix

The RACI Matrix shall be used when:

* Establishing governance responsibilities.
* Defining ownership for new AI initiatives.
* Resolving accountability questions.
* Supporting governance committees.
* Performing internal audits.
* Reviewing governance processes.

Where conflicting responsibilities exist, the designated **Accountable** role shall have final decision-making authority.

---

# 3. RACI Definitions

| Code  | Definition                                                                                                                        |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R** | Responsible — Performs the activity.                                                                                              |
| **A** | Accountable — Ultimately accountable for the successful completion of the activity. Only one role should normally be Accountable. |
| **C** | Consulted — Provides expertise or input before decisions are made.                                                                |
| **I** | Informed — Receives updates regarding the outcome or status of the activity.                                                      |

# 6. Governance Principles

The following principles apply to all governance activities:

* Every governance activity shall have one clearly identified Accountable role.
* Responsibilities shall align with delegated authority.
* Governance decisions shall be supported by appropriate consultation.
* Accountability cannot be delegated to Artificial Intelligence.
* Governance responsibilities shall be reviewed periodically as the Enterprise AI Governance Program evolves.
