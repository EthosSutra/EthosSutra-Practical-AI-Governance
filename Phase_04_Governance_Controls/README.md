# Phase 04 – Governance Controls

## Overview

Phase 04 establishes the governance control framework for the EthosSutra Enterprise AI Governance Reference Implementation.

This phase defines the enterprise policies, standards and operational governance controls required to ensure that Artificial Intelligence systems remain secure, accountable, transparent and compliant throughout their operational lifecycle.

While previous phases establish the AI system, inventory and risk profile, this phase defines **how GlobalBank Corporation governs AI in practice** through policies, responsibilities, human oversight and controlled operational processes.

Collectively, these documents form the Enterprise AI Governance Policy Suite for **GlobalBank Nexus**.

---

# Phase Objectives

The objectives of this phase are to:

* Establish enterprise AI governance policies.
* Define acceptable and responsible AI usage.
* Govern enterprise data throughout the AI lifecycle.
* Maintain meaningful Human Oversight over AI-assisted activities.
* Clearly assign governance responsibilities.
* Govern changes to AI systems through a structured approval process.
* Strengthen regulatory compliance and audit readiness.
* Promote responsible AI adoption across GlobalBank Corporation.

---

# Governance Architecture

The Governance Controls phase is organized into six complementary governance documents.

| Document                        | Purpose                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI_Governance_Policy.md**     | Establishes the enterprise AI governance framework, guiding principles and governance objectives.                                   |
| **Acceptable_Use_Policy.md**    | Defines permitted and prohibited use of GlobalBank Nexus by authorized users.                                                       |
| **Data_Handling_Guidelines.md** | Governs how enterprise information is created, classified, processed, shared, retained and disposed of throughout the AI lifecycle. |
| **Human_Oversight_Plan.md**     | Defines how meaningful human judgement, intervention and accountability are maintained for AI-assisted activities.                  |
| **RACI_Matrix.md**              | Assigns governance responsibilities and accountability across business, governance and technology functions.                        |
| **AI_Change_Management.md**     | Governs the evaluation, approval, implementation and monitoring of changes affecting GlobalBank Nexus.                              |

Together, these documents establish the governance operating model supporting the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.
