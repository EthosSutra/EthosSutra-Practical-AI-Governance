# Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way GlobalBank Corporation delivers services, supports business operations and enables decision-making across the enterprise. While AI introduces significant opportunities to improve efficiency, innovation and customer experience, it also introduces new operational, regulatory, security, ethical and reputational risks that require structured governance.

To ensure that AI technologies are deployed responsibly, securely and in alignment with the organization's strategic objectives, GlobalBank Corporation has established this Enterprise AI Governance Policy as the highest-level governance document within its AI Governance Program.

Developed as part of the Enterprise AI Governance implementation led by **EthosSutra**, this policy defines the governance principles, organizational responsibilities, decision-making authority and mandatory governance requirements that apply to the design, development, acquisition, deployment, operation and retirement of AI systems across the enterprise.

The policy establishes a common governance framework that enables business units, technology teams, risk functions, compliance, information security, internal audit and executive leadership to manage AI consistently throughout its lifecycle.

This document serves as the governing authority from which all supporting AI governance standards, procedures, controls and operational guidance are derived.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a consistent enterprise-wide governance framework for Artificial Intelligence that enables GlobalBank Corporation to develop, acquire and operate AI systems in a responsible, secure, transparent and compliant manner.

The policy defines the governance expectations applicable to all AI systems and establishes the principles, responsibilities and governance requirements necessary to:

* Support responsible adoption of Artificial Intelligence.
* Protect customers, employees and business operations.
* Manage AI-related risks throughout the system lifecycle.
* Promote ethical and trustworthy AI practices.
* Ensure compliance with applicable regulatory obligations.
* Strengthen executive oversight and organizational accountability.
* Maintain audit-ready governance evidence.
* Enable continuous improvement of the Enterprise AI Governance Program.

This policy represents GlobalBank Corporation's formal commitment to governing Artificial Intelligence as a strategic business capability rather than solely as a technology initiative.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Artificial Intelligence systems owned, developed, acquired, operated or managed by GlobalBank Corporation.

The policy is applicable across the complete AI lifecycle, including:

* Business use case identification.
* Solution design.
* Data acquisition and preparation.
* Model development.
* Model validation.
* Deployment.
* Production operations.
* Continuous monitoring.
* Incident management.
* Change management.
* System retirement.

The policy applies regardless of whether AI capabilities are:

* Developed internally.
* Procured from third-party vendors.
* Delivered as Software-as-a-Service (SaaS).
* Integrated through APIs.
* Embedded within enterprise platforms.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, third-party service providers and business partners who design, develop, operate, administer or use AI systems on behalf of GlobalBank Corporation.

# 4. Enterprise AI Governance Principles

The following principles govern every AI system operated by GlobalBank Corporation.

These principles are mandatory and shall be reflected within all supporting governance standards, controls, procedures and operational practices.

---

## POL-001 — Governance by Design

AI governance shall be integrated throughout the complete AI lifecycle and shall not be treated as a post-deployment compliance activity.

---

## POL-002 — Risk-Based Decision Making

Governance activities shall be proportionate to the level of business, operational, regulatory and ethical risk presented by each AI system.

---

## POL-003 — Human Accountability

Business accountability for AI-assisted decisions shall remain with authorized personnel.

Artificial Intelligence shall support decision-making but shall not replace organizational accountability.

---

## POL-004 — Transparency

AI systems shall be sufficiently documented to enable stakeholders to understand their intended purpose, governance controls, limitations and operational responsibilities.

---

## POL-005 — Security and Privacy

AI systems shall protect confidential information through appropriate technical, organizational and administrative safeguards throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## POL-006 — Fairness and Responsible AI

AI systems shall be designed, implemented and monitored to reduce bias, promote fairness and support ethical business practices.

---

## POL-007 — Continuous Monitoring

AI systems shall be continuously monitored to identify governance failures, performance degradation, emerging risks and changes requiring governance intervention.

---

## POL-008 — Auditability

Governance activities shall produce sufficient documentation and evidence to demonstrate compliance with organizational policies, regulatory obligations and internal control requirements.

---

## POL-009 — Continuous Improvement

The Enterprise AI Governance Program shall be periodically reviewed and enhanced to reflect evolving business requirements, regulatory developments, technological advances and lessons learned from operational experience.

# 5. Enterprise AI Governance Operating Model

The Enterprise AI Governance Program operates through a structured governance model that integrates strategic oversight, operational governance, risk management and independent assurance across the complete AI lifecycle.

The operating model ensures that governance activities are embedded within business and technology processes rather than being performed as isolated compliance exercises.

The model is based upon four governance layers.

| Governance Layer             | Primary Responsibility                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strategic Governance         | Establish AI strategy, governance direction and organizational accountability.               |
| Operational Governance       | Govern the implementation and day-to-day operation of AI systems.                            |
| Risk & Compliance Governance | Identify, assess, monitor and manage AI-related risks while ensuring regulatory compliance.  |
| Independent Assurance        | Validate governance effectiveness through monitoring, testing and internal audit activities. |

Together, these governance layers provide continuous oversight throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## 5.1 Enterprise AI Governance Lifecycle

Every AI system shall progress through the Enterprise AI Governance Lifecycle established by GlobalBank Corporation.

The lifecycle consists of the following governance phases.

1. Strategy & Foundation
2. AI Registration & Inventory
3. AI Risk Management
4. Governance Controls
5. Responsible AI & Human Oversight
6. Validation & Assurance
7. Operations & Monitoring
8. Incident & Change Management
9. Audit & Evidence Management
10. Framework Alignment
11. Continuous Improvement
12. Governance Reporting & Executive Oversight

Progression between phases shall require completion of mandatory governance activities and approval by the appropriate governance authority.

Effective AI governance requires clearly defined ownership and accountability across business, technology and governance functions.

Responsibilities include:

* Approving enterprise AI risk appetite.

* Approving critical risk acceptance decisions.
* Monitoring AI governance maturity.
* Providing executive oversight of responsible AI practices.

## 6.2 Chief Risk Officer

The Chief Risk Officer is accountable for enterprise AI risk governance.

Responsibilities include:

* Sponsoring the Enterprise AI Governance Program.
* Approving enterprise AI governance policies.
* Reviewing enterprise AI risk reporting.
* Approving residual risk acceptance within delegated authority.
* Escalating significant AI risks to executive leadership.

# 7. Enterprise AI Risk Management Policy

AI risk management shall be integrated into every stage of the AI lifecycle.

GlobalBank Corporation shall maintain a documented, repeatable and evidence-based AI risk management process that supports informed business decision-making.

The Enterprise AI Risk Management process shall include:

* AI Risk Identification
* AI Risk Assessment
* Enterprise Risk Register
* Risk Treatment Planning
* Risk Acceptance
* Continuous Risk Monitoring
* Periodic Risk Reassessment

Risk management activities shall remain aligned with the organization's approved enterprise risk appetite.

---

## POL-010 — Enterprise AI Risk Assessment

Every enterprise AI system shall complete an approved AI Risk Assessment before production deployment.

---

## POL-011 — Enterprise Risk Register

Every identified AI risk shall be recorded within the Enterprise Risk Register and assigned an accountable Risk Owner.

---

## POL-012 — Risk Treatment

High and Critical AI risks shall have documented treatment plans before production deployment.

---

## POL-013 — Risk Acceptance

Residual AI risks shall only be accepted by authorized governance bodies in accordance with delegated approval authority.

---

## POL-014 — Continuous Risk Monitoring

Enterprise AI risks shall be continuously monitored throughout the operational lifecycle.

# 8. Enterprise AI Governance Control Framework

The Enterprise AI Governance Control Framework establishes the methodology used by GlobalBank Corporation to design, implement, operate, monitor and continuously improve governance controls.

Governance controls represent the operational safeguards that translate governance policy into day-to-day business practice.

Every governance control shall:

* Support one or more policy requirements.
* Reduce one or more identified AI risks.
* Produce measurable governance outcomes.
* Generate audit-ready evidence.
* Be periodically tested.
* Be continuously monitored.
* Be assigned to an accountable Control Owner.

Execution of governance controls shall produce sufficient evidence to demonstrate operational effectiveness during internal and external audits.

# 9. Human Oversight Policy

GlobalBank Corporation recognizes that Artificial Intelligence is designed to support human decision-making rather than replace organizational accountability.

Appropriate human oversight shall be maintained throughout the lifecycle of every AI system to ensure that business decisions remain transparent, explainable and aligned with legal, regulatory and ethical obligations.

The level of human oversight shall be proportionate to the potential impact of the AI system and the level of organizational risk identified during the Enterprise AI Risk Assessment.

Human oversight shall not be treated as a manual approval step alone. It represents an ongoing governance function that includes supervision, intervention, escalation and accountability.

---

## 9.1 Human Oversight Objectives

Human oversight activities shall ensure that:

* AI recommendations are reviewed where required.
* Business accountability remains with authorized personnel.
* AI limitations are understood by users.
* Unexpected AI behaviour is identified and escalated.
* High-risk AI activities remain subject to appropriate human intervention.
* AI-assisted decisions remain consistent with GlobalBank policies and regulatory obligations.

---

## 9.2 Human Oversight Requirements

GlobalBank Corporation shall implement human oversight mechanisms appropriate to the classification and risk profile of each AI system.

Human oversight activities may include:

* Review before AI-generated recommendations are executed.
* Approval workflows for high-impact business decisions.
* Exception handling and escalation procedures.
* Manual intervention during abnormal AI behaviour.
* Periodic review of AI outputs.
* Executive oversight for material AI risks.

---

### P-018 — Human Accountability

Human accountability shall remain with authorized business personnel regardless of the level of AI automation.

---

### P-019 — Human Intervention

Authorized personnel shall have the ability to suspend, override or terminate AI-assisted activities when necessary to protect customers, employees, business operations or regulatory compliance.

---

### P-020 — Escalation

Material AI governance concerns shall be escalated through established governance channels without undue delay.

---

# 10. AI Lifecycle Governance

AI governance shall be integrated throughout the complete lifecycle of every enterprise AI system.

Governance activities shall begin before an AI solution is developed or acquired and shall continue until the system is formally retired.

The Enterprise AI Governance Lifecycle consists of the following stages.

| Lifecycle Stage   | Primary Governance Activities                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Business Planning | AI use case identification, business justification and stakeholder engagement. |
| Design            | Architecture review, governance planning and security requirements.            |
| Development       | Data governance, model development and technical documentation.                |
| Validation        | Model testing, bias assessment, privacy assessment and governance review.      |
| Deployment        | Governance approval, implementation of controls and production readiness.      |
| Operations        | Monitoring, incident management, performance measurement and oversight.        |
| Change Management | Governance review of material system changes.                                  |
| Retirement        | Controlled decommissioning, record retention and governance closure.           |

Governance requirements shall apply throughout every stage of this lifecycle.

---

### P-021 — Lifecycle Governance

Every enterprise AI system shall follow the approved Enterprise AI Governance Lifecycle before entering production.

---

### P-022 — Production Readiness

No AI system shall be deployed into production until mandatory governance activities have been completed and documented.

---

### P-023 — Governance Review

Material changes to an AI system shall trigger a governance review to determine whether additional risk assessment, validation or executive approval is required.

---

# 11. Compliance and Regulatory Alignment

GlobalBank Corporation is committed to maintaining an AI Governance Program that aligns with applicable legislation, industry standards and recognized governance frameworks.

The Enterprise AI Governance Program shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued alignment with evolving regulatory obligations.

The governance program has been designed to support alignment with:

* NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
* ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System
* ISO 31000 Risk Management
* ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Management
* EU AI Act
* Internal GlobalBank policies and enterprise risk management requirements

Alignment with these frameworks does not replace organizational accountability. GlobalBank Corporation remains responsible for ensuring that governance activities are appropriate for its business operations and risk profile.

---

### P-024 — Regulatory Compliance

Enterprise AI systems shall comply with all applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

---

### P-025 — Governance Documentation

Governance documentation shall be maintained in sufficient detail to demonstrate compliance during internal reviews, regulatory examinations and independent audits.

---

### P-026 — Evidence Retention

Governance records and supporting evidence shall be retained in accordance with GlobalBank Corporation's Records Management Policy and applicable legal requirements.

# 12. Policy Compliance

Compliance with this policy is mandatory.

All employees, contractors, consultants and third parties acting on behalf of GlobalBank Corporation shall comply with the requirements defined within this policy.

Business leaders remain accountable for ensuring that governance requirements are implemented within their respective areas of responsibility.

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

* Governance remediation activities.
* Increased governance oversight.
* Suspension of AI deployments.
* Internal disciplinary actions where appropriate.
* Regulatory notification where legally required.

### P-027 — Mandatory Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through governance reviews, control testing, monitoring activities and independent assurance processes.

# 13. Policy Exceptions and Waivers

GlobalBank Corporation recognizes that exceptional business circumstances may require temporary deviations from defined governance requirements.

Any exception shall:

* Be formally documented.
* Include a business justification.
* Include an assessment of associated AI risks.
* Define compensating controls where appropriate.
* Receive approval from the designated governance authority.
* Include an approved review and expiration date.

Policy exceptions shall remain subject to ongoing monitoring and periodic reassessment.

---

### P-028 — Exception Approval

No governance exception shall become effective without documented approval from the appropriate governance authority.

---

### P-029 — Temporary Waivers

Temporary waivers shall be reviewed before expiration to determine whether they should be extended, modified or withdrawn.
