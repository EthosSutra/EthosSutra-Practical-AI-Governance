# EthosSutra – Practical AI Governance

> Turning AI Governance Principles into Practice.

EthosSutra – Practical AI Governance is an open initiative dedicated to making AI Governance practical, accessible and implementation-focused.

This repository brings together enterprise-grade governance artifacts, documentation, templates and real-world implementation guidance aligned with leading AI governance frameworks and industry best practices.

Rather than focusing on theory alone, this project demonstrates how AI Governance can be designed, documented and implemented throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## Repository Roadmap

This repository will be built and released incrementally. Each phase represents a key capability required to establish and operate an effective AI Governance program.

The roadmap includes:

- **Phase 01 — Strategy & Foundation**
- **Phase 02 — AI Registration & Inventory**
- **Phase 03 — AI Risk Management**
- **Phase 04 — Governance Controls**
- **Phase 05 — AI Documentation**
- **Phase 06 — Validation & Assurance**
- **Phase 07 — Operations & Monitoring**
- **Phase 08 — Third-Party Governance**
- **Phase 09 — Audit & Control Assurance**
- **Phase 10 — Regulatory Alignment**
- **Phase 11 — Knowledge Base**
- **Phase 12 — Governance Assessment & Dashboard**

Each phase will be published as it is completed, reviewed and ready for public release.

## Guiding Principles

- Practical over theoretical
- Documentation before automation
- Governance by design
- Transparency and continuous improvement
