# Phase 10 – Regulatory Alignment

## Overview

Enterprise AI Governance cannot rely on internally defined controls alone.

Organizations must demonstrate that their governance program aligns with internationally recognized frameworks and regulatory requirements. This alignment provides confidence that governance practices are consistent with industry best practices, management system standards and legally enforceable obligations.

Phase 10 establishes the Regulatory Alignment Framework for GlobalBank Corporation.

The framework demonstrates how a single Enterprise AI Governance Program supporting GlobalBank Nexus aligns simultaneously with:

- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) 1.0**
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)**
- **European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act)**

Rather than implementing separate governance programs for each framework, GlobalBank Corporation harmonizes governance capabilities into a unified operating model that supports risk management, management system conformance and regulatory compliance through shared governance processes, documentation, evidence and assurance activities.

This phase provides complete traceability from international framework requirements to enterprise governance processes, repository artifacts, operational evidence, enterprise tools and compliance validation activities.

The Regulatory Alignment Framework enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

- Demonstrate alignment with globally recognized AI governance frameworks.

# Core Regulatory Alignment Principles

This phase is built upon the following principles:

- Governance capabilities should be implemented once and reused across multiple frameworks.
- Regulatory obligations shall be translated into operational governance processes.
- Every framework requirement shall be supported by documented implementation and objective evidence.
- Compliance shall be validated through independent assurance activities.
- Governance harmonization reduces duplication while strengthening audit readiness.
- Regulatory alignment shall be continuously reviewed as AI systems and legal requirements evolve.
