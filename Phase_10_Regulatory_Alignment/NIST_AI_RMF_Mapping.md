# Executive Summary

The NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) provides a voluntary, risk-based approach for governing, managing and continuously improving trustworthy AI systems.

This document demonstrates how GlobalBank Corporation has implemented the principles and outcomes of the NIST AI RMF within the governance framework supporting GlobalBank Nexus.

Rather than describing the NIST AI RMF itself, this mapping provides traceability between NIST AI RMF requirements, enterprise governance controls, repository artifacts, operational evidence and accountable owners.

The objective is to demonstrate that AI governance activities are not only documented but implemented, measurable and independently verifiable.

This document supports internal governance, executive reporting, audit readiness and regulatory assurance.

          │
          ▼

          │
          ▼

          │
          ▼

          │
          ▼
Close Gap

Gap assessments shall be documented within the enterprise governance improvement register and reviewed by the AI Governance Committee.
