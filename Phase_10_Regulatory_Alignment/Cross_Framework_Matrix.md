# Executive Summary

Enterprise AI Governance rarely aligns with a single framework.

Organizations are simultaneously expected to demonstrate responsible AI governance, operational management system maturity and regulatory compliance.

GlobalBank Corporation therefore operates a unified Enterprise AI Governance Framework that harmonizes governance activities across NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the European Union AI Act.

Rather than maintaining three independent compliance programs, governance controls are implemented once and mapped across multiple frameworks through standardized governance processes, repository artifacts, operational evidence and continuous assurance activities.

This document provides executive-level traceability showing how a single governance activity satisfies multiple international AI governance frameworks while reducing duplicated governance effort and improving audit readiness.
