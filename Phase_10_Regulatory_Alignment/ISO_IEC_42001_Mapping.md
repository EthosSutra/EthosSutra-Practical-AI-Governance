# Executive Summary

ISO/IEC 42001 establishes the world's first certifiable Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS), providing organizations with a structured framework for governing AI responsibly through documented processes, leadership accountability, operational controls, performance evaluation and continual improvement.

This document demonstrates how GlobalBank Corporation has implemented the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001 within the Enterprise AI Governance Framework supporting GlobalBank Nexus.

Unlike a traditional clause-to-clause comparison, this document establishes end-to-end traceability between ISO requirements, enterprise management system processes, governance artifacts, operational evidence, responsible process owners and compliance status.

The objective is to demonstrate that AI governance is embedded into organizational management processes and supported by objective evidence suitable for certification assessments, internal audits and regulatory inspections.

The purpose of this document is to demonstrate organizational conformance with ISO/IEC 42001 by mapping each applicable management system requirement to implemented governance processes, supporting documentation and operational evidence.

# 14. Lessons Learned

| Observation | Recommendation |
|------------|----------------|
| Organizations often focus on documentation rather than operational execution | Audit processes as well as documents |
| Leadership engagement is a key success factor | Ensure executive participation in governance reviews |
| Competence and awareness are frequently underestimated | Maintain role-based AI governance training programs |
| Continual improvement is a certification expectation, not an optional activity | Track and verify all corrective actions |
| Strong traceability between clauses, processes, evidence and records significantly improves audit outcomes | Maintain an integrated AI Management System repository |
