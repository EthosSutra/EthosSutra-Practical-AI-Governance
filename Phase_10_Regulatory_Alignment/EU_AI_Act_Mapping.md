# Executive Summary

The European Union Artificial Intelligence Act establishes legally enforceable obligations for organizations developing, deploying and operating Artificial Intelligence systems within the European Union.

Unlike voluntary governance frameworks, the EU AI Act requires organizations to demonstrate legal compliance through documented governance processes, operational controls and objective evidence.

This document demonstrates how GlobalBank Corporation has implemented the applicable requirements of the EU AI Act for GlobalBank Nexus by establishing traceability between legal obligations, enterprise governance processes, repository artifacts, operational evidence, responsible process owners and compliance status.

The objective is to provide a practical compliance implementation framework capable of supporting regulatory inspections, internal compliance reviews, executive reporting and external assurance activities.

# 13. Lessons Learned

| Observation | Recommendation |
|------------|----------------|
| Legal compliance cannot rely on documentation alone | Validate every obligation through operational evidence and independent assurance |
| High-risk AI obligations evolve with system changes | Reassess compliance after significant model, data or business updates |
| Regulators focus on implementation, not intentions | Ensure every legal obligation is supported by demonstrable processes and records |
| Human oversight remains one of the most scrutinized obligations | Regularly test approval workflows, override mechanisms and reviewer competence |
| Strong legal traceability accelerates regulatory inspections | Maintain clear links between articles, governance processes, evidence and accountable owners |
