# Executive Summary

Third-party AI suppliers introduce operational, security, privacy, regulatory and business risks that extend beyond the enterprise boundary.

Before integrating any external AI service into GlobalBank Nexus, GlobalBank Corporation performs structured vendor due diligence to verify that suppliers satisfy enterprise governance, security and compliance expectations.

This document establishes the standard due diligence framework used to collect evidence, validate supplier capabilities and determine whether an AI supplier is suitable for enterprise use.

Vendor Due Diligence is performed before procurement approval, contract execution and production integration.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish a standardized enterprise process for conducting due diligence on AI suppliers before onboarding.

The due diligence process enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

- Understand supplier capabilities.
- Validate AI governance maturity.
- Review security and privacy practices.
- Verify regulatory compliance.
- Evaluate operational resilience.
- Support risk-based procurement decisions.
- Produce audit-ready supplier evidence.

---

# 2. Scope

This framework applies to all external suppliers providing AI-related products or services including:

- Foundation model providers
- Cloud AI platforms
- Embedding model providers
- Vector database providers
- AI observability platforms
- AI evaluation platforms
- AI security solutions
- Managed AI services
- AI consulting partners
- External AI APIs

---

# 3. Due Diligence Objectives

GlobalBank Corporation establishes the following objectives.

## DD-001 — Supplier Legitimacy

Verify that the supplier is a legitimate and financially stable organization capable of supporting enterprise operations.

---

## DD-002 — AI Governance Maturity

Assess whether the supplier has established responsible AI governance, accountability and operational controls.

---

## DD-003 — Security Assurance

Validate the supplier's cybersecurity capabilities, certifications and operational security controls.

---

## DD-004 — Privacy & Data Protection

Verify how customer data, prompts, outputs and enterprise information are collected, processed, retained and deleted.

---

## DD-005 — Regulatory Compliance

Confirm alignment with applicable regulations, industry standards and contractual obligations.

---

## DD-006 — Operational Resilience

Assess the supplier's ability to maintain reliable service through disaster recovery, incident response and business continuity planning.

# 17. Enterprise Best Practices

| Best Practice | Business Value |
|--------------|----------------|
| Perform due diligence before any procurement commitment | Prevents unmanaged AI supplier risk |
| Evaluate AI governance alongside traditional security controls | Provides comprehensive supplier assurance |
| Require evidence for every supplier claim | Strengthens audit readiness and trust |
| Review suppliers periodically after onboarding | Detects governance drift and emerging risks |
| Include AI-specific questions in every due diligence exercise | Addresses risks unique to AI services |
| Maintain complete evidence for every assessment | Supports regulatory inspections and internal audits |

---

# 18. Lessons Learned

| Observation | Recommendation |
|------------|----------------|
| Traditional vendor reviews rarely evaluate AI-specific risks | Extend due diligence with AI governance criteria |
| Foundation model providers change rapidly | Review model updates and service changes regularly |
| Prompt retention practices vary significantly | Validate contractual data handling commitments |
| AI governance maturity differs widely across vendors | Use standardized assessment criteria |
| Due diligence is not a one-time activity | Reassess critical suppliers annually or after material changes |
