# Phase 08 – Third-Party Governance

## Overview

Modern enterprise AI systems rarely operate in isolation.

GlobalBank Nexus depends on an ecosystem of external AI suppliers including foundation model providers, cloud AI platforms, vector databases, observability platforms, security solutions and managed AI services. While these suppliers accelerate AI adoption, they also introduce operational, security, privacy, regulatory and commercial risks that remain outside the direct control of the organization.

This phase establishes the Enterprise Third-Party AI Governance Framework for GlobalBank Corporation.

It provides a structured approach for identifying AI suppliers, performing due diligence, assessing supplier suitability, managing residual risks and maintaining continuous oversight throughout the supplier lifecycle.

Rather than treating suppliers as traditional software vendors, this framework recognizes them as critical participants in the enterprise AI ecosystem whose governance maturity directly influences the security, resilience and trustworthiness of GlobalBank Nexus.

---

# Purpose

The Third-Party Governance phase enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

- Maintain a centralized inventory of all AI suppliers.
- Perform structured AI-specific vendor due diligence.
- Evaluate supplier governance maturity using standardized assessment criteria.
- Identify and manage residual third-party AI risks.
- Support secure procurement and onboarding decisions.
- Monitor supplier performance throughout the contractual lifecycle.
- Produce audit-ready evidence supporting regulatory compliance and enterprise governance.

# Third-Party Governance Domains

The framework continuously evaluates external AI suppliers across the following governance domains.

| Domain | Objective |
|--------|-----------|
| AI Governance | Evaluate responsible AI practices, governance maturity and accountability. |
| Technical Capability | Assess architecture, scalability, interoperability and AI service quality. |
| Information Security | Validate cybersecurity controls, certifications and operational resilience. |
| Privacy & Data Protection | Review data processing, retention, residency and protection of enterprise information. |
| Regulatory Compliance | Verify alignment with applicable regulations, contractual obligations and industry standards. |
| Operational Resilience | Assess service availability, disaster recovery, incident response and business continuity. |
| Commercial & Financial | Evaluate supplier stability, licensing, commercial viability and long-term sustainability. |
| Third-Party Risk | Monitor residual risks introduced through external AI dependencies and supply chain relationships. |

# Key Enterprise Principles

This phase is built on several governance principles:

- Every external AI supplier must be identified before integration.
- Evidence shall be collected before approval decisions are made.
- Supplier approval shall be based on objective assessment rather than commercial preference.
- Residual risks shall be documented, owned and continuously monitored.
- Critical AI suppliers shall undergo periodic reassessment throughout the contractual lifecycle.
- Governance evidence shall remain traceable from supplier onboarding through supplier offboarding.
