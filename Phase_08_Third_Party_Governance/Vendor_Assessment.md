# Executive Summary

Following completion of AI Vendor Due Diligence, GlobalBank Corporation performs a formal Vendor Assessment to determine whether an external AI supplier satisfies enterprise governance, security, privacy, operational and regulatory expectations.

Unlike Due Diligence, which focuses on collecting evidence, the Vendor Assessment evaluates that evidence using standardized assessment criteria, assigns an overall supplier rating and supports an informed onboarding decision.

The outcome of this assessment determines whether the supplier is approved, approved with conditions or rejected for use within GlobalBank Nexus.

This document provides a repeatable, risk-based assessment methodology that supports procurement decisions, regulatory compliance and enterprise AI governance.

The purpose of this document is to establish a standardized enterprise framework for evaluating AI suppliers before production onboarding.

# 13. Enterprise Vendor Scoring Model

Each assessment domain is scored using a standardized five-level maturity scale.

| Score | Rating | Description |
|------:|---------|-------------|
| 5 | Excellent | Exceeds enterprise expectations with mature controls and demonstrated best practices. |
| 4 | Good | Meets enterprise requirements with only minor improvement opportunities. |
| 3 | Acceptable | Meets minimum onboarding requirements but requires monitoring. |
| 2 | Weak | Significant control weaknesses requiring remediation before onboarding. |
| 1 | Unacceptable | Critical deficiencies preventing supplier approval. |

# 19. Enterprise Best Practices

| Best Practice | Business Value |
|--------------|----------------|
| Separate evidence collection from decision-making | Improves governance independence and objectivity |
| Use weighted scoring rather than subjective judgement | Ensures consistent supplier evaluations |
| Document every approval decision with supporting rationale | Strengthens audit readiness |
| Apply compensating controls for identified weaknesses | Enables controlled onboarding of strategic suppliers |
| Reassess critical AI suppliers annually or after material changes | Maintains ongoing supplier assurance |
| Align vendor assessments with enterprise risk appetite | Supports consistent governance decisions |

---

# 20. Lessons Learned

| Observation | Recommendation |
|------------|----------------|
| AI suppliers evolve rapidly through model and platform updates | Perform reassessments after major releases |
| Strong security does not always indicate mature AI governance | Evaluate AI governance independently |
| Residual risk is unavoidable | Document risk acceptance and mitigation strategies |
| Vendor assessments should be evidence-based | Avoid approval decisions based solely on marketing claims |
| Governance committees require concise executive summaries | Present assessment results through dashboards and risk ratings |
