# Executive Summary

An Enterprise AI Governance Assessment delivers value only when assessment findings are translated into measurable governance improvements.

The Enterprise AI Governance Findings & Action Plan establishes a structured framework for documenting governance observations, validating supporting evidence, identifying root causes, prioritizing remediation activities and monitoring corrective actions through to closure.

Unlike traditional audit reports that conclude with recommendations, this framework manages the complete governance improvement lifecycle by ensuring that findings are owned, tracked, validated and independently verified before closure.

The framework enables GlobalBank Corporation to strengthen governance maturity, improve regulatory readiness and establish a culture of continual improvement across the GlobalBank Nexus AI Governance Program.

---

# 1. Purpose

The Findings & Action Plan enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

- Document governance findings consistently.
- Classify findings according to business impact.
- Perform structured root cause analysis.
- Prioritize remediation activities.
- Assign ownership and accountability.
- Track corrective action progress.
- Validate remediation effectiveness.
- Support executive reporting.
- Drive continual governance improvement.

---

# 2. Governance Improvement Philosophy

The objective of a governance assessment is not to produce findings.

The objective is to improve Enterprise AI Governance capability.

Every finding should result in one or more measurable improvements that reduce organizational risk, strengthen governance maturity and improve regulatory readiness.

Assessment therefore represents the beginning of the improvement lifecycle rather than its conclusion.

# 4. Findings Classification Framework

Assessment findings are classified according to governance impact and organizational significance.

| Classification | Description | Typical Response |
|---------------|-------------|------------------|
| Critical | Immediate threat to legal compliance, operational resilience or organizational trust. | Executive escalation and immediate remediation. |
| High | Significant governance weakness with elevated business risk. | High-priority corrective action. |
| Medium | Governance weakness requiring planned improvement. | Scheduled remediation. |
| Low | Minor improvement opportunity. | Continuous improvement activity. |
| Observation | Recommendation that strengthens governance maturity without representing a control failure. | Consider during future governance planning. |

Finding classification determines remediation priority and executive reporting requirements.

# 6. Root Cause Analysis Framework

Effective remediation requires understanding why a governance weakness occurred.

Root causes should be evaluated across multiple dimensions.

| Root Cause Area | Typical Indicators |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| People | Insufficient knowledge, unclear responsibilities or resource constraints |
| Process | Undefined, inconsistent or ineffective governance processes |
| Technology | Missing automation, inadequate tooling or technical limitations |
| Governance | Weak oversight, poor accountability or unclear decision-making |
| Training | Inadequate governance awareness or capability development |
| Culture | Limited governance adoption or organizational resistance |

Corrective actions should address root causes rather than symptoms.

# 10. Corrective Action Validation

Completion of an action does not automatically close a finding.

Every corrective action shall be independently validated to confirm that:

- The agreed remediation has been fully implemented.
- Governance controls are operating effectively.
- Supporting evidence is complete and verifiable.
- The original risk has been reduced to an acceptable level.
- No unintended governance gaps have been introduced.

Validation activities may include:

- Document review
- Evidence inspection
- Process walkthroughs
- Stakeholder interviews
- Control testing
- Operational observation

Independent validation strengthens confidence in governance improvements.

---

# 11. Finding Closure Criteria

A governance finding may only be closed when all closure requirements have been satisfied.

| Closure Requirement | Validation Objective |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Corrective action completed | Planned remediation fully implemented |
| Root cause addressed | Underlying issue eliminated or reduced |
| Evidence validated | Supporting documentation independently verified |
| Control effectiveness confirmed | Governance control operating as intended |
| Residual risk accepted | Remaining risk formally reviewed and approved |
| Closure approval obtained | Authorized governance owner confirms completion |

Findings that fail validation shall remain open until all closure requirements have been satisfied.

# 16. Enterprise Best Practices

Successful governance remediation programs consistently apply the following practices:

- Address root causes rather than symptoms.
- Assign clear ownership for every corrective action.
- Define measurable success criteria before implementation.
- Validate improvements independently.
- Track remediation through executive dashboards.
- Escalate overdue high-risk findings promptly.
- Reassess governance capabilities after significant remediation activities.
- Capture lessons learned to prevent recurring governance weaknesses.
