# Phase 02 – AI Registration and Inventory

## Executive Summary

Following the establishment of the strategic, business and technical foundation in Phase 01, GlobalBank Corporation begins the operational implementation of its enterprise AI governance program.

The first operational activity in any mature AI governance program is the identification, registration and classification of AI systems operating within the organization.

An organization cannot govern, assess or monitor an AI system that has not been formally identified.

For this reason, GlobalBank Corporation has established a centralized AI Registration and Inventory process as the authoritative entry point into its AI governance lifecycle.

As part of the AI governance implementation engagement, **EthosSutra** has designed this phase to ensure that **GlobalBank Nexus** is formally documented, registered and classified before progressing to AI risk assessment, governance controls and regulatory compliance activities.

The governance artifacts produced during this phase establish the official enterprise record for GlobalBank Nexus and provide complete traceability throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

# Phase Objective

The objective of this phase is to formally register GlobalBank Nexus within the enterprise AI governance program and establish a governed inventory that supports accountability, traceability, risk management and regulatory oversight.

Completion of this phase ensures that GlobalBank Corporation maintains an authoritative record of the AI system, its business purpose, ownership, operational status and governance classification.

This information becomes the primary reference for all governance activities performed throughout the remainder of the AI lifecycle.

---

# Consulting Engagement Context

Following completion of the Strategy and Foundation phase, EthosSutra initiated the operational governance implementation for GlobalBank Nexus.

This phase documents how GlobalBank Corporation formally identifies and governs its enterprise AI platform by establishing standardized governance records, registration procedures and inventory management practices.

These artifacts enable consistent governance across business units while providing the evidence required for audit, regulatory reporting and executive oversight.

# Governance Artifacts

This phase establishes the operational governance baseline through the following artifacts.

| Governance Artifact | Purpose                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI Use Case Intake  | Captures the business justification, intended purpose, stakeholders and governance requirements for the AI system.           |
| AI System Register  | Establishes the official enterprise record for GlobalBank Nexus and its governance attributes.                               |
| AI Inventory        | Maintains a centralized inventory of enterprise AI systems and governance metadata.                                          |
| AI Classification   | Classifies the AI system based on business criticality, operational impact, regulatory exposure and governance requirements. |

Together, these artifacts create the authoritative governance record for GlobalBank Nexus.

# Governance Outcome

Upon completion of this phase, GlobalBank Corporation will have established:

* A formally approved AI use case.
* An official enterprise AI system registration.
* A governed AI inventory.
* A documented AI classification.
* Clearly assigned business and technical ownership.
* End-to-end traceability across the AI governance lifecycle.

These deliverables enable GlobalBank Corporation to proceed confidently into enterprise AI risk management and governance control implementation.

---

# Governance Rationale

AI governance begins with visibility.

Organizations cannot manage AI risks, assign accountability or demonstrate regulatory compliance without maintaining a complete understanding of the AI systems operating within their environment.

By establishing standardized registration and inventory processes, GlobalBank Corporation ensures that every governed AI system enters the governance lifecycle through a consistent, controlled and auditable process.

The governance artifacts produced during this phase provide the operational foundation upon which risk assessments, governance controls, monitoring and audit activities are built.

---

# Framework Alignment

The governance activities performed during this phase support key requirements defined within internationally recognized AI governance frameworks, including:

* NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Govern and Map Functions)
* ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System
* EU AI Act governance and transparency requirements

Rather than maintaining separate records for each framework, GlobalBank Corporation maintains a single governed inventory that supports multiple governance and regulatory obligations.
