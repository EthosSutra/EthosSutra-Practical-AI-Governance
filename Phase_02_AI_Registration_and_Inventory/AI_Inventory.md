# Executive Summary

The Enterprise AI Inventory provides GlobalBank Corporation with a centralized and continuously maintained record of Artificial Intelligence systems operating across the organization.

The inventory establishes enterprise-wide visibility into AI deployments, enabling governance teams to understand what AI systems exist, where they operate, who owns them and what governance obligations apply.

As part of the AI governance implementation engagement, EthosSutra has established the AI Inventory as the primary operational mechanism for maintaining AI system visibility throughout the governance lifecycle.

At the time of this implementation, **GlobalBank Nexus** is the first enterprise AI platform formally governed under the GlobalBank AI Governance Program.

Future AI systems will be incorporated into this inventory using the same governance process established within this repository.

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Inventory enables GlobalBank Corporation to:

* Maintain visibility of enterprise AI systems.
* Establish governance accountability.
* Support AI lifecycle management.
* Enable enterprise risk management.
* Demonstrate regulatory compliance.
* Support internal and external audits.
* Facilitate executive reporting.
* Monitor AI portfolio growth.

The inventory represents the authoritative catalogue of AI systems governed within the organization.

# Inventory Governance Attributes

Each AI system maintained within the Enterprise AI Inventory shall include, at a minimum, the following governance attributes.

| Governance Attribute | Description                                             |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI System Identifier | Unique identifier assigned by the AI Governance Office. |
| Business Owner       | Executive accountable for the AI system.                |
| Technical Owner      | Individual responsible for technical operation.         |
| Business Unit        | Organizational function responsible for the system.     |
| AI System Type       | Classification of the AI capability.                    |
| Lifecycle Stage      | Current operational maturity of the AI system.          |
| Risk Classification  | Governance risk level assigned to the AI system.        |
| Human Oversight      | Indicates whether human review is mandatory.            |
| Regulatory Impact    | Applicable regulatory obligations.                      |
| Governance Status    | Current governance lifecycle status.                    |

These governance attributes provide consistent visibility across the enterprise AI portfolio.

# Inventory Maintenance

The AI Governance Office is responsible for maintaining the Enterprise AI Inventory.

Updates shall occur when:

* A new AI system is proposed.
* An AI system enters production.
* Ownership changes.
* Business purpose changes.
* Risk classification changes.
* Significant architectural changes occur.
* An AI system is retired.
* Regulatory obligations materially change.

Each inventory update shall be traceable through approved governance records.

---

# Governance Controls

The following governance controls apply to the Enterprise AI Inventory.

* Every AI system must possess a unique AI System Identifier.
* Every AI system must have an assigned Business Owner.
* Every AI system must have an assigned Technical Owner.
* Every AI system must complete AI Use Case Intake before registration.
* Every AI system must complete risk assessment prior to production deployment.
* Every AI system must maintain current governance documentation.
* Every inventory record shall be reviewed at least annually or upon material change.

These controls ensure the inventory remains accurate, complete and auditable.

---

# Governance Rationale

Enterprise AI governance depends upon complete visibility of AI systems deployed across the organization.

Without a governed inventory, organizations cannot reliably determine which AI systems require risk assessments, monitoring, regulatory reporting or governance oversight.

The Enterprise AI Inventory establishes a single source of truth for AI system visibility and provides the foundation for governance reporting, executive oversight and audit readiness.
