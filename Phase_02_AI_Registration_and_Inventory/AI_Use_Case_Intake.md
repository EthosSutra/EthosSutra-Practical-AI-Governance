# Business Problem Statement

GlobalBank Corporation operates across multiple banking functions where employees rely on large volumes of policies, procedures, regulatory guidance and operational knowledge to perform business activities.

Traditional knowledge retrieval methods are time-consuming, inconsistent and difficult to maintain as enterprise information continues to expand.

Additionally, customer support teams, compliance officers and operational staff require timely access to accurate information while maintaining compliance with internal governance requirements and external regulatory obligations.

GlobalBank Nexus has been proposed to address these challenges by providing governed AI-assisted knowledge retrieval, intelligent decision support and workflow assistance.

---

# Business Objectives

The proposed AI system will:

* Improve employee productivity.
* Accelerate access to enterprise knowledge.
* Enhance customer service quality.
* Improve operational consistency.
* Support fraud investigation activities.
* Assist regulatory and compliance teams.
* Reduce manual knowledge search effort.
* Enable responsible enterprise AI adoption.
