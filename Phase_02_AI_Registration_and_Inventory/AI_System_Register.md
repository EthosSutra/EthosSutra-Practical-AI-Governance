# Executive Summary

The AI System Register serves as the official enterprise record for AI systems governed under GlobalBank Corporation's Enterprise AI Governance Program.

Following approval of the AI Use Case Intake, GlobalBank Nexus has been formally registered as an enterprise AI system and assigned a unique governance identifier.

The AI System Register establishes the authoritative governance record for the platform, documenting ownership, operational status, governance scope, lifecycle stage and key governance attributes.

This register supports governance oversight, regulatory reporting, audit readiness and enterprise-wide visibility of AI systems operating within GlobalBank Corporation.

# Governance Rationale

The AI System Register provides GlobalBank Corporation with a centralized and authoritative record of enterprise AI systems operating within the organization.

Maintaining a governed register ensures that every AI system has assigned ownership, documented governance status and complete lifecycle traceability.

The register also supports regulatory reporting, audit evidence collection and executive oversight by providing a consistent governance record throughout the AI system lifecycle.
