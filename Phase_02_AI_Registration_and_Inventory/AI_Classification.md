# Executive Summary

AI classification is the process of determining the governance obligations applicable to an AI system based on its intended purpose, business impact, operational context, regulatory exposure and associated risks.

Following completion of the AI Use Case Intake, AI System Registration and Enterprise AI Inventory, GlobalBank Corporation has performed an initial governance classification of **GlobalBank Nexus**.

This classification establishes the level of governance, oversight, assurance and monitoring required throughout the AI system lifecycle.

The outcome of this assessment determines the governance controls, risk management activities and regulatory obligations that apply to GlobalBank Nexus.

---

# Classification Methodology

GlobalBank Corporation classifies AI systems using a risk-based governance methodology that considers multiple dimensions rather than relying on a single factor.

Classification considers:

* Business criticality
* Customer impact
* Regulatory impact
* Data sensitivity
* Decision influence
* Human oversight requirements
* Third-party AI dependency
* Operational complexity
* Enterprise deployment scope

This methodology ensures governance requirements remain proportionate to the AI system's overall risk profile.

---

# Governance Rationale

The purpose of AI classification is not to determine whether an AI system may be deployed.

Its purpose is to determine **how the AI system must be governed**.

By classifying GlobalBank Nexus according to business impact, operational significance and governance requirements, GlobalBank Corporation ensures that governance activities remain proportionate, risk-based and aligned with organizational objectives.

This classification serves as the governance baseline for all subsequent phases of the Enterprise AI Governance Program.
